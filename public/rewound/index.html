Your phone, a 90s camcorder

Rewound turns your phone into the camcorder you had in 1998. Point, press REC, it is on tape. Every look runs live in the viewfinder, and your photos and videos come out the same.

  • 5,000 looksVHS, camcorder, 8mm, film stocks, digicam, pixel, fisheye, light leaks, glitch and more.
  • What you see is what you getEvery look is live in the viewfinder. Photos and videos match it exactly.
  • Real camcorder feelDate stamp, tape texture and a camcorder viewfinder with REC and timecode.
  • Make each look yoursPress and hold a look to change its speed and intensity. Every look remembers its own settings.
  • Photos, video, burstsPhotos, videos, Half Frame and Photobooth. A timer and a series of shots. Trim and export with the look burned in.
  • Private by designNo account. Your shots stay on your phone and go to Photos only when you save them.

Rewound is free with a set of free looks. Rewound PRO unlocks every look, the pro tools and 4K video export on supported devices.
