FAQ

Questions? Answers.

When does Rewound come out?

Soon, on the App Store and Google Play. On launch day this page links to both stores.

Is Rewound free?

Yes. Rewound is free to use with a set of free looks, with no ads and no watermark. Rewound PRO unlocks every look, the settings of each look and 4K export.

Do the looks work on videos too?

Yes. Every look runs live in the viewfinder and works the same on photos and videos. Half Frame and Photobooth are for photos.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The looks run on your phone, so you can shoot anywhere. You only need a connection to buy or restore Rewound PRO.

Can I change a look?

Yes. Press and hold a look to slow its motion down or freeze it and set its intensity. With PRO you also get the look's own settings. Every look remembers how you left it.

Does it work with the front camera?

Yes. Both cameras, with every look, for photos and videos.

How often do new looks come?

Every month a new tape of looks arrives for Rewound PRO. This month it is VICE.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your shots never leave your phone.

Where are my photos and videos?

In Rewound’s own gallery on your phone. They go to your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos or share them. Deleting the app deletes the gallery, so save what matters.

My phone gets warm when I film. Is that normal?

Yes. Live looks, 4K and 60 fps use a lot of processing power. Take breaks, keep the phone out of direct sun, and pick a lighter look or a lower resolution if it gets very hot.

4K is not available on my phone.

4K video export depends on your device and is part of Rewound PRO. Other devices use 1080p.
