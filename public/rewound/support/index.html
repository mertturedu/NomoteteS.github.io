Rewound support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to us and we answer personally, usually within two working days.

Email nomotetes.onetrue@icloud.com

Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the Rewound version (Settings in the app), and what you were doing. A short description or a screen recording helps.

Common questions

How do I restore my PRO purchase?

Open Rewound, go to the paywall (tap any PRO look) or Settings, and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. If nothing is found, write to us with the email or receipt from Apple.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rewound on your iPhone, or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund a purchase ourselves.

Where are my photos and videos?

In Rewound’s own gallery on your phone. They go to your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos or share them. Deleting the app deletes the gallery, so save what matters.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your shots never leave your phone.

Why does the app ask for the microphone, Photos or location?

Microphone: to record sound with videos. Photos (add only): to save a shot when you ask. Location is optional and only tags your shots with a place, stored on your phone. Every permission can be changed in your phone’s Settings.

My phone gets warm when I film. Is that normal?

Yes. Live looks, 4K and 60 fps use a lot of processing power. Take breaks, keep the phone out of direct sun, and pick a lighter look or a lower resolution if it gets very hot.

4K is not available on my phone.

4K video export depends on your device and is part of Rewound PRO. Other devices use 1080p.

How do I delete my data?

Your content and settings live only on your phone, so deleting the app removes them. For subscription or usage records held by our providers, write to us (see above).
