Política de privacidad

Last updated: 17 November 2026

El texto legal completo está en inglés. La versión en inglés es la que vincula legalmente.

Short version: your photos and videos stay on your phone, there is no account, and we do not track you. This is the full text of what Rewound does with data. “We” is Mert Turedu, the developer and publisher of Rewound.

1. Your photos and videos stay on your phone

Rewound applies its filters on your device and keeps your shots in the app’s own gallery on your phone. We never upload, view, copy or store your photos and videos on any server of ours.

A shot goes to your Photos library only when you choose Save to Photos or Share.

2. What we collect

There is no account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or contacts, and we do not use an advertising identifier.

Subscription status: when you buy Rewound PRO, the store (Apple or Google) and our subscription provider RevenueCat tell the app whether PRO is active. RevenueCat works with an anonymous identifier made for the app’s installation, your purchase receipts and the country and currency of the store. It does not receive your photos, your videos or your contacts.

Usage and crash data: anonymous events about which features are used (for example that a filter was chosen or a video was exported) and crash and performance reports, so that we can fix problems and improve the app. These events do not contain your photos, your videos, the words you typed into the app’s text components or your exact location.

None of this is used to track you across other apps or websites, to profile you or to show you advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. What stays on your phone

Your shots, their original captures (when “Keep originals” is on), your settings, your favourite looks, the layout of the screen components, the date and time you set, the texts you typed and the place tags of your shots are stored on your phone only. If you delete the app they are deleted with it.

The date and time you set in Filter & Components are only text drawn on your own pictures and videos. They do not change your phone’s clock.

4. Permissions

Camera: to show the live preview and to shoot photos and videos. Nothing from the camera leaves your phone.

Microphone: to record sound with your videos. Photos work without it.

Photos (add only): to save a shot to your library when you ask us to.

Location (while the app is open, optional): to tag your shots with the place where they were taken (coordinates and the name of the city, found with Apple’s map service). The place is stored with the shot on your phone only and is never sent to us. You can leave it off.

Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): the app may ask whether it may measure how our campaigns perform. Rewound does not track you today, and every feature works whichever you choose.

You can change any of these at any time in your phone’s Settings or in Settings → Permissions in the app.

5. Who we share data with

Only the service providers the app needs to work: Apple or Google (purchases, maps for the place name), RevenueCat (subscription status) and our analytics and crash-report providers. They process data for us, under their own privacy terms, and only for these purposes. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the European Economic Area and the United States. Where the law asks for it, they rely on approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep data

Your content and settings live on your phone for as long as you keep the app. Subscription, analytics and crash records are kept by our providers for as long as needed to run and improve the app and to meet legal duties, and are then deleted or made anonymous.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Union, the United Kingdom or Türkiye under the KVKK) you may ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to its use, ask for a copy, or complain to your data protection authority.

Because we do not know who you are (there is no account), we may ask you for the purchase or device details we need to find your records. Write to us at the address below.

9. Security

Your content never leaves your phone, which is the strongest protection we can offer. Our providers protect the little data they handle with the usual technical measures. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that it is.

10. Children

Rewound is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example when a new service is added). The date above shows the last change. If a change is important we will say so in the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: nomotetes.onetrue@icloud.com
